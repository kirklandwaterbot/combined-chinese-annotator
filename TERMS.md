## Purpose and features

Combined Chinese Annotator is a browser extension for reading and learning Chinese. It provides annotations, dictionary lookup, saved vocabulary, review, text conversion, Reader mode, speech, and stroke practice. Features and compatibility can vary by browser, website, and version.

It is a learning aid. Readings, definitions, conversions, and generated speech may contain errors. Check important text against reliable sources before relying on it.
